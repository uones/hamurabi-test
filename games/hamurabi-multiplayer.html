Hamurabi Multiplayer

Players:

· Year of 10

Trade offer: positive acres = you buy land from them (you pay price × acres); negative acres = you sell land to them (they pay you price × |acres|). Price is bushels per acre.

Declare war: choose a player and how many people to send as your army. The defender uses all their people. Both sides take casualties; you gain acres from the defender based on their losses.

Share this link with the next player. Copy the link and send it to them; when they open it, they take their turn.
